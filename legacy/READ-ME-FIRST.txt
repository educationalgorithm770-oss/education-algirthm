SEPARATE STEP - IMPORTANT SECURITY FIX (edit .env on the server)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Your live .env currently has APP_ENV=development. That leaves several
development-only shortcuts switched ON for real visitors.

In File Manager, open public_html/.env and change these two lines:

    FROM:  APP_ENV=development
    TO:    APP_ENV=production

    FROM:  ALLOW_DEV_PAYMENT=false
    TO:    ALLOW_DEV_PAYMENT=false

Save. No file upload needed for this - just edit those two lines.
