Help & Transparency

Frequently Asked Admissions Questions

What are the live cohort timings? ▼
Our flagship cohorts conduct live interactive classes on Saturdays and Sundays (10:00 AM • 1:00 PM IST or 2:00 PM • 5:00 PM IST). In addition, daily doubt-clearing sessions and peer coding channels on Discord run throughout the week. High-definition recordings are accessible 24/7 on your Student LMS dashboard.
Are there any prerequisites for beginners or non-CS students? ▼
No prior coding experience is required for the Java Full Stack track. Month 1 starts from foundational principles: variables, operators, memory allocation, conditionals, loops, and logic building before progressing into object-oriented architecture and database systems.
How does the 1-on-1 Code Review process work? ▼
For every weekly assignment and sprint deliverable, you submit a GitHub Pull Request (PR). Our senior faculty and tech mentors perform line-by-line code reviews, evaluating your time complexity, naming conventions, design patterns, and unit test coverage.
What placement and interview assistance is provided? ▼
Students who complete 80%+ quizzes and successfully defend their Capstone E-Commerce project receive 1-on-1 resume optimization, LinkedIn profile reviews, 3 full-length technical mock interviews, and direct interview referrals to our hiring network of 120+ tech organizations.
Are payment installments and EMI options available? ▼
Yes. You can pay via debit/credit cards, UPI, net banking, or choose flexible 3-month no-cost EMI options directly at checkout via our Razorpay payment gateway.
