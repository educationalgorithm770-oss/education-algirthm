Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about batch timings, mentorship, and certifications.

What if I miss a live class? +
All live lectures are recorded in high definition and made available on your Student LMS dashboard within 2 hours of class completion, along with downloadable PDF notes and code snippets.
Are the certificates industry-recognized? +
Yes! Every certificate issued by Education Algorithm contains a permanent cryptographic UUID and a verifiable QR code that employers and recruiters can verify on our public verification portal.
Do I need prior coding experience? +
Our tracks are structured from absolute fundamentals (variables, data types, control flow) and progressively advance to complex topics like microservices, recursion, and distributed databases.
How does the 1-on-1 mentorship work? +
You are paired with an experienced industry mentor who conducts weekly 1-on-1 code reviews, doubt-solving sessions, and mock interview preparations to ensure you stay on track.
